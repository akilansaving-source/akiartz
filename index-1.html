About the Artist

A Practice Rooted in
Observation

I work with oil and mixed media to trace the moments between seeing and feeling. Each canvas begins as an act of looking — at light falling across a room, at colour shifting through seasons, at the quiet architecture of everyday life.

My work has been exhibited across independent galleries, and I take a limited number of commissions each year for collectors who seek something deeply personal.
